# Blackbird identity kit

Blackbird is the private desktop product of «Технологии Товарищества».

## Visual rule

- The bird crossing a dark eclipse is exclusive to the Blackbird master mark.
- Service marks never contain planets, eclipses, orbital rings or birds.
- Service marks use one compact symbol, black metal, a silver edge and at most one pale-blue status point.
- Marks must remain readable at 20–24 px and are delivered as transparent PNG files.

## Assets

- `blackbird-master.png` — approved master mark.
- `services/atlas.png` — four-point direction mark.
